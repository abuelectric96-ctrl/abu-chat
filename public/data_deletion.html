Data Deletion Instructions

How to permanently delete your data from ABU-chat.

ABU-chat respects your right to control your personal data. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time, free of charge. This page describes how.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email, phone, password hash)
  • All connected Instagram account information and access tokens
  • All auto-reply rules and configurations you created
  • All comment and reply logs we stored on your behalf
  • All billing records (kept only as required by tax law)

Method 1 — Delete from your dashboard (recommended)

  1. Sign in at abu-chat.uz
  2. Open Settings → Account
  3. Click Delete my account
  4. Confirm by typing DELETE
  5. Your data is removed immediately. Backup copies are purged within 30 days.

Method 2 — Email request

If you cannot access your dashboard, send an email from the address registered on your account to:

Email: abuelectric96@gmail.com
Subject: Data Deletion Request
Body: Please include your registered email and Instagram username.

We will confirm and complete deletion within 30 days.

Method 3 — Remove via Facebook

You can remove ABU-chat's access to your Instagram at any time:

  1. Open Facebook → Settings → Business Integrations
  2. Find ABU-chat in the list
  3. Click Remove

This revokes our access tokens. To also delete the data we already stored, please use Method 1 or 2.

What is not deleted

  • Replies that ABU-chat already posted to your Instagram remain on Instagram. To remove them, delete them in the Instagram app.
  • Anonymized aggregate analytics that cannot be linked back to you may be retained.
  • Records required by law (e.g. tax invoices) are kept for the legally mandated period.

Confirmation

Once deletion is complete you will receive an email confirmation. If you have any questions, contact abuelectric96@gmail.com.

Ma'lumotlarni O'chirish Yo'riqnomasi

ABU-chat'dan ma'lumotlaringizni butunlay o'chirish.

ABU-chat sizning shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringiz ustidan nazoratingizni hurmat qiladi. Akkauntingizni va u bilan bog'liq barcha ma'lumotlarni istalgan vaqtda, bepul, butunlay o'chirishingiz mumkin.

Nimalar o'chiriladi

  • Akkaunt profilingiz (ism, email, telefon, parol hash)
  • Ulangan barcha Instagram akkauntlari va access tokenlari
  • Yaratgan barcha avto-javob qoidalari va sozlamalar
  • Sizning nomingizdan saqlangan barcha komment va javob loglari
  • Hisob-kitob yozuvlari (faqat soliq qonunlari talab qilgan muddatgacha saqlanadi)

1-yo'l — Dashboard orqali (tavsiya qilinadi)

  1. abu-chat.uz ga kiring
  2. Sozlamalar → Akkaunt bo'limini oching
  3. Akkauntimni o'chirish tugmasini bosing
  4. DELETE so'zini yozib tasdiqlang
  5. Ma'lumotlaringiz darhol o'chiriladi. Backup nusxalar 30 kun ichida tozalanadi.

2-yo'l — Email orqali

Dashboard'ga kira olmasangiz, akkauntingiz email manzilidan quyidagiga email yuboring:

Email: abuelectric96@gmail.com
Mavzu: Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish so'rovi
Matn: Akkaunt email'i va Instagram username'ini ko'rsating.

Biz 30 kun ichida tasdiqlab, o'chirishni yakunlaymiz.

3-yo'l — Facebook orqali

ABU-chat'ning Instagram'ingizga ruxsatini istalgan vaqtda olib tashlashingiz mumkin:

  1. Facebook → Settings → Business Integrations
  2. Ro'yxatda ABU-chat'ni toping
  3. Remove tugmasini bosing

Bu access token'larimizni bekor qiladi. Allaqachon saqlangan ma'lumotni ham o'chirish uchun 1 yoki 2-yo'ldan foydalaning.

Nimalar o'chmaydi

  • ABU-chat allaqachon Instagram'ga yuborgan javoblar Instagram'da qoladi. Ularni o'chirish uchun Instagram ilovasidan foydalaning.
  • Sizga bog'lab bo'lmaydigan anonim umumlashtirilgan statistika saqlanishi mumkin.
  • Qonun talab qilgan yozuvlar (masalan, hisob-fakturalar) qonuniy muddat davomida saqlanadi.

Tasdiqlash

O'chirish tugagach email orqali tasdiq xati keladi. Savollar bo'lsa: abuelectric96@gmail.com.
