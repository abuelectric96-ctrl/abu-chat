Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 4, 2026 · Last updated: May 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ABU-chat ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website (abu-chat.uz) and our auto-reply service for Instagram comments (the "Service").

By using ABU-chat, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account information: name, email address, password (stored as a one-way hash), phone number (optional).
  • Payment information: billing details when you subscribe to a paid plan. Payment processing is performed by third-party providers (Click, Payme, Stripe). We do not store full card numbers.
  • Content you create: auto-reply rules, keywords, reply templates, and configuration settings.

1.2 Information from Meta / Instagram

When you connect your Instagram Business or Creator account through Facebook Login, we receive the following from Meta's Graph API, with your explicit permission:

  • Your Instagram user ID, username, and profile picture URL
  • The Facebook Page ID linked to your Instagram account
  • An access token used to read comments and post replies on your behalf
  • Comments left on your Instagram media (text, author username, timestamp, comment ID)
  • Basic information about your Instagram media (post ID, caption) limited to what is required to operate the Service

We do not request, collect, or store your Instagram password. Access is performed solely through Meta's official OAuth flow.

1.3 Information collected automatically

  • IP address, browser type, device information
  • Pages visited, time spent, referrer URL
  • Cookies and similar technologies for session management and analytics

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for the following purposes:

PurposeExample
Provide the ServiceReading new comments, matching keywords, posting replies
Account managementLogin, password reset, billing
CommunicationService updates, support responses, important notices
ImprovementAggregated, anonymized analytics to improve features
Legal complianceResponding to lawful requests by authorities

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only in these limited cases:

  • Meta Platforms: through API calls required to read and post comments on Instagram on your behalf.
  • Payment processors: Click, Payme, Stripe — to process subscriptions.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: e.g. Render, Railway, MongoDB Atlas, AWS — strictly to host and operate the Service.
  • Legal authorities: when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt; we never store plain-text passwords.
  • Access tokens are encrypted at rest using industry-standard encryption.
  • Data is hosted in secure, reputable cloud infrastructure with access controls.
  • We use HTTPS (TLS) for all data in transit.
  • While we apply reasonable safeguards, no system is 100% secure. Use a strong, unique password.

5. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • Comment and reply logs are retained for up to 90 days after creation, then automatically deleted unless you choose to export them.
  • If you delete your account, all personal data and tokens are deleted within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g. accounting records).

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data — request a copy at any time.
  • Correct inaccurate information through your dashboard.
  • Delete your account and associated data — see our Data Deletion page.
  • Disconnect your Instagram account at any time from the dashboard or by removing ABU-chat from your Facebook Business Integrations.
  • Withdraw consent for processing at any time.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication (keeping you logged in) and optional analytics cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser, but core features may not work.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed on servers located outside Uzbekistan (e.g. EU, USA) where our infrastructure providers operate. By using the Service, you consent to such transfers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect changes. Material changes will be notified by email or via the dashboard.

1.2 Meta / Instagram'dan olinadigan ma'lumotlar

Instagram Business yoki Creator akkauntingizni Facebook Login orqali ulaganingizda, sizning roziligingiz bilan Meta Graph API'dan quyidagilarni olamiz:
